The monthly protocol

One month of protocols with room for your own work.

Who we are looking for

  • You are a longevity enthusiast, founder or researcher interested in experimenting with practices that support healthspan.
  • You are already exploring a question, practice or project at the intersection of AI, longevity and human life.
  • You can attend your selected month or week in person in Chiang Mai.
  • You are willing to be challenged and see yourself from a new perspective.

Practitioners, mentors and investors with a clear contribution to the residency are also welcome to apply.

What happens after you apply

  1. 01Tell us about yourself, what you are experimenting with and your preferred stay.
  2. 02You will receive a response within 24 hours.
  3. 03You may be invited to a short conversation with Andrej, online or in person.
  4. 04If it is a mutual fit, we will confirm your place and prepare your stay together.
