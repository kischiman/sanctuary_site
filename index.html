Who we are looking for

  • You are a founder or builder working in health or longevity.
  • You can be in Kobe for the full residency, 10–25 October.
  • You can join up to two hours of online preparation before the stay.
  • You have a product or working prototype residents can use and test.

Hands-on investors with a clear contribution to the residency are also welcome to apply.

What happens after you apply

  1. 01Send a short application about you and your product.
  2. 02You will receive a response within 24 hours.
  3. 03You may be invited to a short conversation with Andrej, online or in person.
  4. 04If it is a mutual fit, we will confirm your place and prepare together.
