About the residency

Two weeks co-living, testing, and fundraising for health-tech in Kobe, Japan.

From 10–25 October, we are staying in the National Strategic Special Zone in Kobe, Japan. The Sanctuary is a real world innovation enclave for 5 residents and will be part of the Mirai Tech Popup City.

Apply to join us if you are a founder or investor with a deep interest in healthspan extension.

Residents will work together toward getting users for their products, connecting with the biotech sector and attracting investment.

A living showcase of the next generation of health tech

For two weeks, we will live and work together, test products and longevity interventions in daily life, support each other's progress, and meet companies and investors in Kobe's biomedical ecosystem.

Testing, accountability, and progress

During the stay, we will share progress updates to hold one another accountable, gather insights, and exchange practical advice on product, marketing, fundraising, and organizational design.

Kobe NSSZ ecosystem access

Field trips and private visits to companies and research organizations within the Kobe Biomedical Innovation Cluster (KBiC).

Tools, supplements, peptides, and N=1 protocols

We will learn from each other how to improve our health through better sleep, nutrition, movement, recovery, self-measurement, and sustainable working rhythms.

Purpose and outputs

What we take with us.

  • Our individual progress in a report
  • Project-testing observations and feedback
  • Insights from workshops and field trips
  • Connections to ecosystem stakeholders and investors

The most useful insights will be synthesized into a case study report with individual and collective learnings. Raw collected data will be stored locally and encrypted.
