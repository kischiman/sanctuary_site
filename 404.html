Purpose and outputs

Two research questions. Everyday life as the setting.

How can human/AI interfaces help us learn while strengthening our own understanding and agency? What changes when we approach life as a game, with self-chosen quests, feedback and shared reflection? We will explore these questions through small, voluntary experiments, grounded in longevity and everyday wellbeing.

  • A shared record of questions, experiments and individual progress
  • Human/AI learning prototypes and participant feedback
  • Reflections on agency, motivation and life as a game
  • Connections with fellow residents and the EthChiangMai community
  • A fundraising-ready case study with prototype feedback and a clearer investor narrative
