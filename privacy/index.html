Privacy & data

How we handle your information.

Last updated: August 2, 2026

The short version

Sanctuary is a small, independently run residency programme. We only collect what we need to review your request to join and to stay in touch with you. We don’t sell your data and we don’t share it with advertisers.

What we collect

  • Email address — so we can respond to your request and, if you opt in, send you updates about future Sanctuaries.
  • Your project description — what you are building and your current bottleneck. Read by the host to assess fit for the cohort.
  • Basic technical data — timestamp of your submission and which part of the page it came from.

Where it’s stored

Your information is stored in an encrypted Postgres database managed by our backend provider. Access is restricted to the Sanctuary host and protected by row-level security policies. Data collected during the residency itself is stored locally and encrypted.

How we use it

  • To review your request and reach out about next steps.
  • If you ticked the box, to occasionally email you about future Sanctuaries and related updates (you can opt out any time).
  • To understand demand and improve the programme.

Analytics

This site uses the Meta Pixel to measure how people find it and whether they go on to apply. It sets cookies and reports page views and form submissions to Meta, which may use that for advertising measurement and targeting. It does not receive the contents of your application or your question — only that a submission happened. Any tracker-blocking extension or browser setting will stop it.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your data.
  • Pass your email address, application or question on to advertisers.

Your rights

You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time. Email andrej@deepwork.studio and we’ll respond within 14 days.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to how we handle your data we’ll update this page and, where appropriate, notify you by email.
